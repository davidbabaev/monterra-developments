[REPLACE] Monterra Ridge sits on six acres in East Austin, a short drive from the
river and the city centre. We bought the site in 2025 and spent the first year on
grading and drainage work before any framing began.

[REPLACE] The plan puts forty-eight townhomes and duplexes around a single
landscaped green rather than along a through road. Every home opens onto that
green on one side and a private fenced yard on the other. Parking sits behind the
homes, so the shared space stays clear of cars.

[REPLACE] Construction started in early 2026. We expect to hand over the first
homes in the third quarter of 2027, with the remaining phases following through
the winter.
