[REPLACE] The Larkin is a thirty-two unit condominium building in the Highlands,
north-west of downtown Denver. It replaced a single-storey warehouse that had been
empty for over a decade.

[REPLACE] The ground floor holds three retail units facing the street, which keeps
the block active in the evening. Above them are four floors of one and two bedroom
homes, with a shared roof deck at the top.

[REPLACE] We completed the building in 2024. Every home sold within nine months of
the sales launch, and the retail units were leased before handover.
